About TEBS

TEBS is a 100% Black-owned (Pty) Ltd registered company operating across South Africa. We provide integrated strategic, professional, and developmental services to organisations, institutions, government entities, and entrepreneurs.

Our team consists of qualified facilitators, assessors, moderators, instructional designers, recruitment specialists, business consultants, human capital practitioners, and project managers with extensive experience across higher education, skills development, recruitment, employment services, business development, organisational support, and community development sectors.

Leadership Excellence

Mr. Lufuno Godfrey Tshikhudo

Chairman, Top Employment and Business Solutions

With over 21 years of distinguished experience in the South African higher education sector, Mr. Tshikhudo has served in various senior leadership and executive management roles in Student Affairs and Development, Governance, Marketing and Communications, Operations, Institutional Coordination, and Strategic Institutional Support across several public universities.

He has successfully led and contributed to major strategic projects involving universities, government departments, municipalities, private sector stakeholders, development agencies, funding institutions, and community organisations.
