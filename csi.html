Corporate Social Investment

Empowering the Youth of South Africa through Skills, Opportunity, and Hope

Our Initiatives

Bridging the Gap to Economic Participation

Through our CSI initiatives, TEBS partners with various public and private institutions to empower unemployed youth through skills development, employability support, entrepreneurship development, and access to economic opportunities. We are committed to sustainable socio-economic transformation.

Our Core Initiatives

Creating pathways for young people to access their first employment opportunities.

Empowerment Workshops

We organise annual nationwide empowerment workshops and career development programmes offered free of charge to job seekers.

Career Guidance

Providing professional career guidance, work-readiness training, and CV writing support to enhance employability.

Entrepreneurship

Supporting small business development and entrepreneurship to foster self-reliance and economic growth.

Skills Development

Delivering capacity-building workshops in communication, IT literacy, and professional etiquette.

Networking

Connecting job seekers directly with employers and industry stakeholders through dedicated networking events.

Recruitment Access

Facilitating direct recruitment and employment opportunities for graduates and unemployed professionals.

Free Nationwide Empowerment Workshops

As part of our commitment to youth empowerment, TEBS organises annual workshops focusing on interview preparation, CV writing, and recruitment opportunities.
